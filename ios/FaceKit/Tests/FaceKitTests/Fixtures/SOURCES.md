# Test photos

Official NASA astronaut portraits from Wikimedia Commons. All are works of the U.S. federal government
(NASA) and in the **public domain**, as stated on each file page. They were cropped around the face and
scaled to 360 px wide (`make_fixtures` step in `ios/tools/README.md`); `reference.json` holds the numbers
`ios/tools/reference.py` computed from exactly these files.
